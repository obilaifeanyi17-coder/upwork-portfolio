# AI Lead Follow-Up & Workflow Automation for Real Estate

**Stack:** n8n, Make
**Client type:** real-estate team

## The problem

Enquiries arrived after hours and sat until someone opened an inbox. In real estate the first
reply usually wins the viewing, so every slow reply handed the lead to a competitor.

## The build

An enquiry becomes a trigger rather than an email nobody is watching. The automation sends an
immediate first reply, asks the qualifying questions a human would ask, and hands the qualified
lead over with the conversation already attached.

## The result

Every enquiry gets a real first response within minutes, at any hour, and nothing sits unread in
an inbox overnight.
