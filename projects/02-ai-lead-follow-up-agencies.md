# AI Lead Follow-Up for Real Estate & Agencies

**Stack:** GoHighLevel, n8n
**Client type:** agencies and real-estate teams

## The problem

Leads were captured into a CRM that nobody worked fast enough. Follow-up depended on a person
remembering, so it varied by whoever was on shift.

## The build

GoHighLevel holds the record; n8n does the chasing. New leads are answered instantly, scored on
the answers they give, and pushed into the right pipeline stage with reminders attached so no lead
depends on memory.

## The result

Follow-up runs the same way at 9am and 9pm, and the pipeline reflects reality because every step
writes back to it.
