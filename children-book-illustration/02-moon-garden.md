# Milo and the Moon Garden

<small>Concept project · Fantasy adventure picture book · Ages 4–8</small>

## Story concept
Milo discovers a hidden garden that only appears under moonlight. Each plant responds to a different sound, and Milo must listen carefully to help the garden bloom again.

## Illustration direction
Whimsical nighttime fantasy with deep blue-green foliage, silver moonlight and luminous pastel flowers. Combine painterly foliage with clean character silhouettes so each spread remains easy for young readers to follow.

## Character design
- **Milo:** A thoughtful child explorer in a mustard raincoat, teal boots and a small satchel.
- **Luma:** A tiny moth guide with pale mint wings and a soft glow.
- **The Moonflowers:** Tall, expressive flowers with distinct shapes and gentle personalities.

## Key scenes to illustrate
1. Milo noticing a silver glow behind an old garden gate.
2. Luma guiding Milo along a winding path of glowing leaves.
3. A wide garden spread showing flowers that respond to humming, tapping and clapping.
4. Milo listening closely to a drooping flower.
5. The garden glowing with color as the final flower opens.

## Deliverables to develop
- Main character sheet and expression studies
- Moth and flower supporting-character designs
- Five storyboarded scenes / sample spreads
- Cover illustration concept
- Layered working files and agreed export formats

## Production notes
Use consistent lighting direction across spreads to make the moonlit environment feel connected. Reserve calm areas for text and check that small details remain legible at the intended book size.
