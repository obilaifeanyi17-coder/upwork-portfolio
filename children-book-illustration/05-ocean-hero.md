# The Ocean Needs a Hero

<small>Concept project · Environmental picture book · Ages 5–8</small>

## Story concept
When a young turtle encounters litter along the shore, a child named Kemi joins a beach clean-up and learns how everyday choices can help protect ocean life.

## Illustration direction
Vibrant coastal scenes with layered turquoise water, sandy neutrals, coral accents and lush tropical greens. Balance engaging underwater details with a calm, readable composition suitable for young readers.

## Character design
- **Kemi:** A curious child in a sun hat, bright T-shirt and practical beach shoes.
- **Tavi:** A young sea turtle with a distinctive shell pattern and expressive eyes.
- **Ocean friends:** Small fish, crabs and seabirds used as supporting visual characters.

## Key scenes to illustrate
1. Kemi spotting Tavi near the shoreline.
2. An underwater view showing the turtle navigating a littered area.
3. Kemi learning how rubbish can affect marine animals.
4. Friends and neighbors working together during a beach clean-up.
5. A hopeful final spread showing a cleaner shore and healthy ocean habitat.

## Deliverables to develop
- Kemi and Tavi character sheets
- Underwater and beach environment studies
- Five sample picture-book scenes
- Cover illustration concept
- Print-ready illustration exports

## Production notes
Depict marine wildlife respectfully and avoid presenting unsafe clean-up actions as child activities. Keep environmental messaging age-appropriate and have factual conservation details reviewed by a suitable subject-matter source.
