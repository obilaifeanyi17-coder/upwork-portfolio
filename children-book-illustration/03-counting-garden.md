# A Garden Full of Numbers

<small>Concept project · Educational early-reader picture book · Ages 3–5</small>

## Story concept
A curious child explores a community garden, counting seeds, seedlings, butterflies and vegetables from one to ten. The story introduces numbers through a friendly visual search.

## Illustration direction
Bright, cheerful and uncluttered. Use fresh greens, sunny yellows, tomato reds and sky blues. Shapes should be simple and recognizable, with strong contrast between the objects being counted and the background.

## Character design
- **Tomi:** An enthusiastic young gardener wearing green overalls and a sun hat.
- **Grandma Ada:** A patient gardening companion who encourages observation.
- **Garden visitors:** Butterflies, ladybirds, birds and small garden creatures.

## Key scenes to illustrate
1. Tomi and Grandma Ada preparing the garden bed.
2. One watering can and two tiny seed packets.
3. Three butterflies hovering above the flowers.
4. A busy vegetable patch with clearly countable produce.
5. A final garden panorama inviting children to find and count ten objects.

## Deliverables to develop
- Tomi and Grandma Ada character sheets
- Reusable garden-object illustration set
- Five sample educational page illustrations
- Numbering and text-placement suggestions
- Cover concept and print-ready page-art exports

## Production notes
Keep counting objects visually distinct and avoid hiding objects behind text. Use consistent object scale and a clear reading path. Educational content and final number sequences should be checked by the author or educator.
