# Nia's Big Family Picnic

<small>Concept project · Contemporary family picture book · Ages 4–7</small>

## Story concept
Nia is excited about the family's weekend picnic, but rain changes the plan. With help from relatives, she discovers that a memorable family day is about being together, wherever they are.

## Illustration direction
Warm, lively and character-led. Use a sunny palette of coral, golden yellow, turquoise and leafy green. Build expressive group scenes while keeping each character readable and distinct.

## Character design
- **Nia:** An imaginative child with a bright picnic bag and a favorite sketchbook.
- **Family ensemble:** A varied group of relatives with distinct silhouettes, clothing and expressions.
- **The family dog:** A playful supporting character who adds gentle visual humor.

## Key scenes to illustrate
1. Nia packing fruit, a blanket and her sketchbook.
2. The family arriving at the park as rain clouds gather.
3. Everyone transforming the living room into an indoor picnic.
4. A lively shared meal, games and storytelling.
5. Nia drawing the day as a family memory.

## Deliverables to develop
- Character lineup with expressions and outfit references
- Group-scene composition studies
- Five sample story illustrations
- Cover concept
- Print-ready final artwork exports

## Production notes
Maintain recognizable character proportions and clothing details throughout the book. For group scenes, use clear staging and varied poses so the reader can follow the action without visual clutter.

Character illustration · Family storytelling · Group composition · Color storytelling · Picture-book layout
