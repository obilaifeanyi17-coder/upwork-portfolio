# The Little Star Who Found Her Glow

<small>Concept project · Bedtime picture book · Ages 3–6</small>

## Story concept
A tiny star worries that her light is too small to matter. With encouragement from the moon and a few nighttime friends, she discovers that even a gentle glow can help someone find their way home.

## Illustration direction
A dreamy night-sky palette with indigo, soft lavender, warm gold and muted peach. Use watercolor-inspired digital textures, soft edges and expressive faces. Keep the star's silhouette and facial features consistent from page to page.

## Character design
- **Pip:** A small five-pointed golden star with rounded tips, rosy cheeks and curious eyes.
- **The Moon:** A calm, friendly crescent with a gentle expression.
- **The Fireflies:** Tiny supporting lights that create movement and scale in the night scenes.

## Key scenes to illustrate
1. Pip looking at the brighter stars from a quiet corner of the sky.
2. Pip meeting the moon and explaining why she feels too small.
3. A little fox searching for the path home beneath the trees.
4. Pip shining gently above the forest as the fox spots the way.
5. Pip smiling among the stars, understanding that her light matters.

## Deliverables to develop
- Character turnaround and expression sheet for Pip
- Supporting character sketches
- Storyboard / page thumbnails
- Five sample picture-book illustrations
- Front-cover concept and print-ready artwork layout

## Production notes
Prioritize clear focal points, warm emotional expressions and enough negative space for short blocks of story text. Final trim size, bleed, color profile and resolution should be confirmed against the publisher's printer specifications.
